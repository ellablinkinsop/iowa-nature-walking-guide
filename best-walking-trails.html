Looking for convenient, scenic, and well-maintained walking paths right in the Iowa City area? Our team has ranked the top routes based on safety, pavement quality, scenic views, and ease of parking.

1. Iowa River Corridor Trail

Distance: 6.2 miles total
Surface: Paved asphalt
Difficulty: Easy

Running alongside the Iowa River through the heart of the University of Iowa campus and connecting to Coralville, this flat, fully paved trail is ideal for casual walkers, parents with strollers, and morning joggers. You will pass iconic locations such as the Art Building West, the Hancher Auditorium grounds, and several historic bridges. Multiple access points offer public restrooms and benches.

2. Hickory Hill Park Outer Loop

Distance: 3.1 miles
Surface: Crushed limestone & Dirt
Difficulty: Moderate

Located on the northeast side of Iowa City, Hickory Hill Park provides a tranquil forest escape without leaving the city limits. The outer loop winds through dense hardwood timber, across small timber bridges over Ralston Creek, and past restored prairie stands. It features moderate elevation gains that provide a good light workout while remaining accessible to walkers of most fitness levels.

3. Terry Trueblood Recreation Area Lake Loop

Distance: 1.8 miles loop
Surface: Paved concrete
Difficulty: Easy

Circling Sand Lake on the south side of Iowa City, this wide concrete loop path provides continuous waterfront views. It is completely flat, smoothly paved, and offers zero traffic hazards, making it one of the most popular walking destinations for families and seniors. The park features ample parking, modern restrooms, and outdoor picnic pavilions near the park lodge.
