Exploring Beyond Johnson County

Eastern Iowa is home to some of the Midwest's most unique state parks, ranging from driftless blufflands to limestone caves and ancient glacial valleys. If you are looking to venture beyond the immediate Iowa City area for a day trip, here are three must-visit state parks within a 90-minute drive.

Maquoketa Caves State Park

Location: Maquoketa, IA (approx. 65 miles northeast)
Highlights: 16 natural caves & limestone bluffs

One of Iowa's most unique geological destinations, Maquoketa Caves features a six-mile trail system winding through towering bluffs, natural stone bridges, and dark caverns. Walkers can explore light hiking loops above ground or equip themselves with flashlights and old clothes to crawl through accessible limestone caves.

Palisades-Dows State Preserve & Palisades-Kepler State Park

Location: Mt. Vernon, IA (approx. 20 miles north)
Highlights: Cedar River cliffs & river valley trails

Dramatically perched along the Cedar River, Palisades-Kepler features rugged limestone palisades jutting above the water. Deep woodland hiking trails follow old river bluffs, offering spectacular autumn leaf views and abundant birdwatching opportunities along five miles of interconnected trails.

Wildcat Den State Park

Location: Muscatine, IA (approx. 45 miles southeast)
Highlights: Pine Creek Grist Mill & sandstone rock formations

Wildcat Den combines rich history with steep, scenic hiking paths. Visitors can tour the historic 1848 Pine Creek Grist Mill before hiking trails that navigate past sandstone formations like Steamboat Rock and Devil's Punchbowl. It offers a moderate challenge with rewarding overlooks.
