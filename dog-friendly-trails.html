Exploring with your four-legged companion is a fantastic way to experience Johnson County. Here are our top-rated dog-friendly routes and local leash guidelines.

🕒 Dog Park Hours & Seasonal Rules

Hours: Open daily from sunrise to sunset.

Seasonal Advice: Off-leash areas remain open during winter months, though water fountains are shut off from November through April to prevent pipe freezing. Be sure to bring your own water for pets during winter walks!

1. Thornberry Dog Park Trail

Area: North Iowa City
Surface: Grass & Packed Dirt
Leash Rule: Off-Leash Fenced Area

Featuring wide open spaces, agility features, and fenced enclosure zones, Thornberry is Iowa City's premier destination for letting dogs run free while enjoying scenic walking paths.
