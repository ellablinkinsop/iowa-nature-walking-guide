Iowa City Dog Hiking Etiquette

All city and county nature trails around Iowa City require dogs to be kept on a physical leash no longer than 6 feet at all times. Always pack out waste, carry adequate water for your pet, and check your dog for ticks after every woodland walk.

Kinnick Signature Trail & Waterworks Park

Leash Required (6ft max)

Waterworks Park offers vast open green space, shaded dirt tracks, and wide crushed-gravel pathways surrounding prairie wetlands. It is ideal for active dogs who enjoy plenty of scent work and quiet trails away from heavy city traffic. Water stations are located near the primary parking lot on Dubuque Street.

Squire Point & Woodpecker Nature Trail

Leash Required | Rocky Terrain

Located just north of Iowa City on Coralville Lake, these interconnected timber paths feature earthen routes, rocky steps, and wooden boardwalks leading to scenic lake overlooks. Dogs love the forested shade and winding paths, though owners should ensure their dogs are nimble enough for occasional steep steps and exposed tree roots.

Thornberry Off-Leash Dog Park Trails

Off-Leash Allowed inside Fenced Area

For dogs that need freedom to run off-leash, Thornberry Dog Park on Foster Road offers dedicated fenced areas, including a special pond area where dogs can swim safely. Surrounding the fenced paddocks are short, paved walking paths where you can stroll while your dog interacts safely within controlled, double-gated zones.
