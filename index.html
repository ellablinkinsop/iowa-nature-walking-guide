Welcome to Prairie & Pines

Welcome to Iowa City's premier online trail resource! Founded in 2024, Prairie & Pines Trail Guides was established by a passionate group of local hikers who wanted to make Eastern Iowa’s natural beauty accessible to everyone. Whether you are looking for a gentle morning stroll along the Iowa River, a rugged afternoon hike through woodland forests, or a dog-friendly park where your pup can burn off energy, we have compiled the detailed maps, photos, and tips you need.

🕒 Park Hours & Seasonality

Operating Hours: City parks are open daily from 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM. State park grounds are accessible from 4:00 AM to 10:30 PM year-round.

Seasonal Highlights:

  • Spring & Summer: Lush canopy trails and wild river scenery along the Iowa River.
  • Fall: Outstanding autumn foliage across Hickory Hill and Lake Macbride.
  • Winter: Paved commuter paths are plowed; dirt trails offer snowshoeing and winter wildlife tracking.
