Located just north of Iowa City near Solon, Lake Macbride State Park features some of the most scenic woodland and shoreline hiking trails in Eastern Iowa.

🕒 State Park Hours & Seasonal Activities

Hours: 4:00 AM – 10:30 PM daily (Iowa DNR official park hours).

Seasonal Highlights: Peak hiking and lake views occur May through October. During winter, the multi-use trails are popular for cross-country skiing, snowshoeing, and bald eagle watching along open lake water near the spillway.

1. Lake Macbride Beach Loop

Distance: 2.5 miles
Surface: Natural Footpath / Packed Earth
Difficulty: Moderate

A beautiful winding trail hugging the shoreline of Lake Macbride, offering stunning water vistas and shaded forest canopy stretches.

2. Osprey Trail

Distance: 1.5 miles
Surface: Crushed Limestone
Difficulty: Easy

An accessible path winding through restored prairie restoration areas and wetlands, perfect for birdwatching and scenic morning walks.

3. Spillway Trail

Distance: 1.2 miles
Surface: Rocky Dirt / Uneven
Difficulty: Moderate

A popular route connecting Lake Macbride to Coralville Lake, leading down to the famous limestone spillway and waterfall areas.
